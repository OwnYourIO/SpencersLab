## Goal
Remove the four duplicate Service manifests that make ArgoCD stamp `Resource
/Service/default/seaweedfs-{filer,master,s3,volume} appeared 2 times among application resources`
on every refresh: `charts/seaweedfs/templates/seaweedfs-{master,s3,filer-http,volume-http}-service.yaml`
hard-name the same Services the upstream subchart emits. Default approach = R1 §Q6 ranked option
(a) (delete the wrapper templates); option (b) (rename the local Services) is the recorded
fallback. The choice and its human-side prerequisites are recorded in DECISIONS.md before edits.
