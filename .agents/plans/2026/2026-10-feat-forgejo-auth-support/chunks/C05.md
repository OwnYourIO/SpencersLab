## Goal

Register the same `gitea`-type External Auth Provider on the GPU-cluster coder
server (coder.spencerslab.com — live-verified: Deployment `default/coder`
ghcr.io/coder/coder:v2.35.2, ingress `coder.spencerslab.com`,
`CODER_ACCESS_URL=https://coder.{{ .Values.domain }}` from
`services/gpu/prod/templates/secrets-coder.yaml`) pointing at the CANONICAL
Forgejo (git.spencerslab.com), and enable token-at-rest encryption there too.
This is the canonical→canonical half of the user's per-Coder pairing: the
community license caps external-auth providers at ONE per coder instance, and
each of the two instances spends its slot on its paired forge.

Independent of C04: zero shared files (gpu umbrella templates/values +
custom-values/gpu vs charts/coder + custom-values/dev). The provider env
contract is the same one verified at v2.35.2 (see C04's Goal); the reloader
controller runs cluster-wide on gpu too (open-webui already carries the
annotation — base deploys it).
