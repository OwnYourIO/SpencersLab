## Goal

Land the two official vLLM Grafana dashboards (`performance_statistics`,
`query_statistics`) in the central Grafana via the repo's proven
`grafana.dashboards` `url:` convention, pinned to the vLLM repo tag **v0.33.0** —

dashboards-only. Logs reaching Loki via `podLogsViaLoki` needs zero new surface
(R5 Q3); GPU-side telemetry is follow-up card #1.
