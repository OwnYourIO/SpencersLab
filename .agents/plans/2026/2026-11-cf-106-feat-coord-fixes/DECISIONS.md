## Conventions
- Sources in `.rulesync/**` are the only edits; `.opencode/{agents,commands,skills}/**` and `AGENTS.md` regenerate (`npx --yes rulesync@37.0.0 generate` ×2 + `--check`) and commit with their sources.
- Hand-managed surfaces never regenerate: `.opencode/plugin/**`, `skills/**`, `.opencode/opencode.jsonc`, `.agents/loops/**`, `.aislop/config.yml`.
- Every `tests/**` write (new suite, re-pin, scope-admit line) belongs to `agent-test`; dev has `tests/**` denied, so a needed pin fix routes back to `test_session` (cf-96 friction ×5).
- Offline proof is the gate: nothing in this ticket may depend on the deployed `dev-boards` build (`wekan-mcp:v87` lacks `preload_board`, `set_card_labels`, `create_list`).
- Stamp rule (cf-96 OQ4): displayed stamps are workspace-local ISO with offset, UTC `Z` when `$TZ` is unresolvable (this workspace: `$TZ` empty → `Z`); epoch-ms values and mtimes are never reformatted.
