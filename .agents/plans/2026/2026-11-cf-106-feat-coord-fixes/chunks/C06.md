size: L   wave: 2   depends_on: [C01]

## Goal
Turn the claim-time preload into the one read the pickup walk actually reasons with: **all** cards grouped swimlane → list (with the titles, not just ids), each card carrying its two ids (the WeKan `_id` and the display `cardNumber` — the "AD-106" number), its labels, its `slug`, its archive flag, and now its **checklist state** — while staying one request and staying lean (no titles, no descriptions, no comments).

The "never the hook" sentence and the probe/absent/failed degrade law stay byte-familiar (`tests/boards-deps` t2 pins their co-location).
- [ ] Docs: `skills/wekan-mcp/SKILL.md` tool description + `skills/wekan-mcp/references/call-shapes.md` preload row; the `cf96-deploy` readback runbook's preload shape check updated.
