- **`unloadTimeout` default 10 s force-kills a ~47 GiB page-locked unload** (RESEARCH contradictions #4): per-model `unloadTimeout: 180` (values-configurable, VERIFY timed-unload on-target), keeping `terminationGracePeriodSeconds: 420 > unloadTimeout + drain`.
