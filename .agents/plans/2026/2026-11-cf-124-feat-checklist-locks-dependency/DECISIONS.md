## Conventions

- This ticket changes a **contract generation** (the board checklist vocabulary) — every copy of the vocabulary moves in the same commit: `.rulesync/skills/boards/SKILL.md` (+ generated mirror), `scripts/provision-board.py`, the rules artifact, `skills/wekan-board-provision/SKILL.md`, `.rulesync/rules/overview.md` → `AGENTS.md`. Copy the landed text, never retype it from memory (the cf-96/cf-106 rule the suites enforce).
- **Arm-level green bars.** The nine pre-existing red arms (across seven suites) listed in the plan's Commands are reported, never silently absorbed and never "fixed" as a side effect.
- **Repo contract ≠ board data.** No acceptance criterion asserts a live-board state. Anything the owner must do on the board is a USER-owned step or a script's owner-run `--apply`.
- **No promise the platform cannot keep.** No text, test or acceptance criterion may depend on a working rule `checkItem`/`uncheckItem` or a rule-set custom-field default — both are proven unavailable at the v12.77 pin and at upstream `master` (RESEARCH §1, §2).
