# C06 — Dependency gate on `related-to`: source-is-the-dependent, sole gating type, pickup-only scope, grouping by inbound edges

size: **L** · wave 2 · depends_on: **C02** · model: `gpu-ai/qwen3.8-flash-next-strata-q4` (both tiers)

## Goal

Replace the shipped "blocking edge set is EXACTLY `blocks` and `is-blocked-by`" law with the owner's
ruling (OQ12/OQ12b → D24): **`related-to` is the ONLY gating type**, the **stored source is the
dependent** (`A →related-to→ B` means *A waits for B*), `blocks`/`is-blocked-by` become
**display-only** (zero such edges are live, so nothing migrates), the gate decides only whether a
card may **start**, and grouping triggers on the claimed card's **inbound** `related-to` edges — with
mutual/ambiguous pairs never gating and always reported.

## Ground truth the text must not overstate

- Upstream `related-to` has **no semantics at all**: it is undirected (`directed:false`, its own inverse), stored only on the drawing card with no inverse twin, and upstream's only edge behaviour is cleanup on a cross-board move (RESEARCH §5). The direction is therefore a **lab convention**, and the whole gate is consumer-side doctrine — the text must say so rather than implying the server enforces it.
- Live board: **four** `related-to` edges (`#56→#135`, `#73→#140`, `#126→#124`, `#140→#126`) and **zero** `blocks`/`is-blocked-by` (EXPLORE). Under the new law #56, #73 and #126 become unpickable at merge (OQ14 accepted; the owner's re-draw list is USER-owned step 5) and `#126↔#140` is the **mutual** pair the tie-break exists for.
- The satisfaction set is unchanged by the ruling: a blocker satisfies when it is **archived** or sits in a list titled **Test** or **Finished** — **Review does not satisfy** (`boards:14`, and D6's "Review is the release point" stays).
- C02 has already landed the authority that **`Executing` membership alone means the card is owned** — C06 cites it in the grouping qualifier instead of the old "under another run's lock" phrasing (`boards:380`), and never re-derives it.
- The gate is one of the preload consumer's consumers (`boards:320`), and its degrade law is unchanged: absent/failed `preload_board` ⇒ per-list reads, gate SKIPPED, note in RESULT — a degraded run must not stall and must not gate on partial data.
- `fixes`/`is-fixed-by`, `duplicates`/`is-duplicated-by` stay non-gating, and `parent_id`/`fixes` stay non-grouping (**C08** lands the follow-up edge right after this chunk; C06 must write the non-gating sentence so C08 doesn't contradict it).
