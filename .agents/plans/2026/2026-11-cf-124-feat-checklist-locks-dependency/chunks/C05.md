## Goal

Write the **mechanics** behind the two genuinely-gated stages **C01** put in the vocabulary and Stage
map, in the shape the owner ruled at approval (**D15** gates + rename degrade; **D28** supersedes the
mint-guard half): the kickoff rename and mint legs become conditional on the card's checked

stays forbidden from touching surfaces the run never created.
