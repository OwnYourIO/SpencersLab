# Decisions

Append-only. Newest at the bottom. Format: date, who (plan|cord|human), decision, alternatives rejected, why.

## Conventions
- Plan artifacts live in this folder; inputs (researched drafts, probes) live in `/tmp/opencode/` on the planner's machine — they are starting points for dev, not committed artifacts.
- "Tests" in this repo = validation scripts under `tests/` (structural/grep/bash -n), `helm lint`/`helm template`, `terraform validate`, and renovate schema checks. There is no unit-test suite (per AGENTS.md "Pipeline specifics").
- Work happens on a feature branch in this worktree; merge/push to main is the user's decision alone.
