# C08: repo-add loop completeness — `exit`→`next` at the three awk sites
size: S   wave: 1   depends_on: []

## Goal

Make the "Add repositories" loop register EVERY URL-shaped dependency row of
EVERY chart, on every fire, in all three jobs. The shipped hardened awk (cf-77
r2) ends its program with `exit` inside the print block, so each per-chart loop
terminates after the first emitted row — multi-URL charts register one repo,
and `cr package` (v1.10.1, lock-driven `downloader.Manager.Build()` per chart)
dies naming the unregistered ones (owner fire: `no repository definition for
https://charts.jetstack.io, https://charts.christianhuth.de`). The fix is the
minimal mechanical swap `exit` → `next` (skip to next record, keep iterating)
at all three byte-identical sites, plus the test-surface truth it falsifies.
Ground truth: EXPLORE.md "Fix-round-2 scout (2026-11-23)" §A0–§A7; ruling D45.
