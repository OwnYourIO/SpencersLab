# C17: One registration token → ten self-registering runners (round 6)
size: M   wave: 1   depends_on: [C16]

## Goal
Owner requirement (verbatim): "a single token from Forgejo that gets used in the
10 runners." Today's C16 shape holds ONE registered runner's `(uuid, token)`
pair in the ESO Secret and mounts it in all 10 pods — Forgejo can never see
more than one row because `ActionRunner.UUID` is a UNIQUE column (RESEARCH
§Round-6 TL;DR 1 [S2]). C17 turns the Secret into a single REUSABLE
instance-scope REGISTRATION token and moves identity minting into the pod:
each ordinal registers itself on its first boot (`POST {runner.url}
/api/v1/admin/actions/runners` — response `{id, uuid, token}`, RESEARCH
§Q2(b) [S14]), persists the identity on its OWN `data` PVC, and re-attaches
(not re-registers) on every later boot. Ten admin rows per release, named
after the pods; jobs still route uniformly because the LABEL payload stays
the identical baked 5-set on every row (D62 — per-ordinal labels on one uuid
would thrash `agent_labels`, RESEARCH Q1 [S4]; that interim idea is corrected
out of the record).
