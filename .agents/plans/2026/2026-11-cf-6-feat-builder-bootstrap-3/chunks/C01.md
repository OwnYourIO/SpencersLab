## Goal

Make the proven D33 `get.helm.sh` TLS-35 class stop killing builds: give the three forgejo-runner
bake fetches a deterministic retry budget (protects this image wherever it is built — lane and
containers-build alike), and (if accepted) cap the dind daemon bridge MTU at the documented 1450
(protects every future TLS-egress build through either runner release, active at the O-4 sync).
