# C02: Item A — infra Forgejo instance + proxy-local routing + leftovers cleanup
size: L   wave: 1   depends_on: []

## Goal
Deploy the second Forgejo instance on the infra cluster from the existing
`charts/forgejo` wrapper (parameterized per decision A8), serve it at
`git.spencerslab.com` (canonical) + `forgejo.spencerslab.com` (alias) through
the infra generic-ingress, route both hosts through proxy-local with
crowdsec-only middleware (decisions A3–A5), wire OIDC + mailer + storage
(decisions A1/A2/A6/A9), and delete the dormant gitea-era leftovers (A7).
