(bucket-C verdict, EXPLORE §C.3): the node layer interpolates the BARE semver

`3713c83d1` (2026-10-20) and why B1's full-fleet publish must wait for this
commit (plan G1 gate). PR1/PR2/PR31 are orthogonal to it — re-runs redden.
