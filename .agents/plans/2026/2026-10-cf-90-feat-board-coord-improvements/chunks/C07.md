size: M   wave: 2   depends_on: [C03]

## Goal
Create the follow-up cards the initial ask defers (card rule: "create the
cards after post research and before writing the tests and code"), following
C03's follow-up protocol (destination **Inbox** in the Backlog swimlane —
batched-round ruling; parent labels documented in-body — the deployed
surface cannot set labels). Existing cards are CITED, never duplicated,
never mutated. Executor: the COORDINATOR itself (boards-skill friction-
follow-up precedent — board writes are the coord's surface; no dev subagent),
scheduled right after the post-research round and before the agent-test
session.

## Acceptance criteria
1. Exactly the CREATE-list cards exist afterwards (expected 8, recomputed
   against the live board at execution time — an entry found already carded
   is cited instead, and the deviation is recorded).
2. Zero duplicates of the CITE-list cards; they are untouched (no move, no
   edit, no comment on them).
3. Every created card carries: appendix content, parent-card reference,
   parent-labels line (minus Research), the five standard checklists with
   the chunk's answers checked.
4. Card #90 has the one ledger comment.
5. RESULT carries the full id table (L1..L12 → created id / cited id).

## Tests (agent-test writes, review runs)

  body carries the three required lines; `list_checklists` + `get_checklist`
  on the sample — the five checklists with the right items checked;
  `list_comments` on card #90 — the ledger comment present.
- agent-test's deliverable for this chunk is the read-back checklist written
  into THIS chunk file's Review notes contract (a runbook, not code).
