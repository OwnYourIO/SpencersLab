# C02: `charts/argocd-hook` + proxy-local service trio (chart, appset entry, crowdsec-only subdomain, secrets)

## Goal

Deploy the relay onto the proxy-local hub the repo's standard way: a bjw-s app-template
chart consuming the C01 image, wired through the full service trio — `charts:` ApplicationSet
entry in `services/proxy-local/prod/values.yaml`, a crowdsec-only public `argocd-hook`
subdomain entry on the hub, and ExternalSecret + Bitwarden secrets with the
`OVERRIDE_VIA_CUSTOM_VALUES` ⇄ `custom-values/proxy-local/prod-values.yaml` 1:1 pairing.
Zero changes to any appset gate, `charts/base`, or other clusters.
