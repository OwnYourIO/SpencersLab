## Goal

Make the property the owner asked about **pinned and legible** instead of merely present: the
detector diffs the whole pushed range, and a run that builds nothing says so. Four behaviour/shape
changes by ruling — `fetch-depth: 0` stays with its why in the comment and a leg (B1/OQ-c); the
first-push fallback announces itself (A4); an empty `before` goes red rather than silently tip-only
(A5, OQ-a); the changed set + endpoints are echoed and an empty push/PR set warns (B4, OQ-e/OQ-k) —
plus the **PR arm moved to a merge-base diff** (B5, OQ-b) and the legs that make a regression of the
owner's exact fear shape (deleting the depth line, or narrowing to `$SHA~1..$SHA`) go red **offline**.
Nothing here re-opens run -1's guard/tag/registry decisions. Commit-only class: nothing is pushed.
